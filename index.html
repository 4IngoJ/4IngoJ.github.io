SideSchedule

My calendar kept losing to every other window. Not anymore.

A calendar sidebar that reserves its strip of screen the way the Dock does. Meeting notes become Markdown files, and one click joins your call.

  • Joins Zoom, Meet, Teams, Webex and FaceTime calls.
  • Six languages, macOS 13 or later.

TalkRemind

You bought a dictation app. But you’re still typing? Not anymore.

A menu-bar app that notices a long stretch of typing and nudges you to use the dictation app you already own. It never transcribes and never reads what you type.

  • Recognises 17 dictation apps; any app with a hotkey works.
  • Four nudge styles, quiet hours and per-app exemptions.
  • macOS 14 or later.

Free for 14 days, then €9.99 once. First 100 buyers across both apps: €5.99.
