SideSchedule · macOS 13+

My calendar kept losing to every other window.

A calendar sidebar that keeps its strip of screen the way the Dock does. Meeting notes become Markdown files, and one click joins your call.

  • Reads Apple Calendar, so iCloud, Google, Exchange and CalDAV work.
  • Joins Zoom, Meet, Teams, Webex and FaceTime calls.
  • Six languages.

TalkRemind · macOS 14+

You bought a dictation app. You still type.

A menu bar app that notices a long stretch of typing and nudges you to use the dictation app you already own. It never transcribes and never reads what you type.

  • Recognises 17 dictation apps. Any app with a hotkey works.
  • Four nudge styles, quiet hours and exemptions per app.
  • Shows how much you dictate compared to typing.

ToneCue · macOS 13+

Your Mac, with the microphone you meant.

A menu bar app that picks your microphone and speakers by situation: a dock, a calendar meeting, an open app. It tells you why it switched, and you can undo it.

  • Scenes with rules. Higher scenes win.
  • Pauses when you pick a device by hand.
  • Works from Raycast too.

What every app comes with.

Price

You pay once

No subscription. After 14 free days the license is yours, for two Macs with SideSchedule or ToneCue and three with TalkRemind.

Account

There is none

Nothing to sign up for. You enter a license key and that’s it.

Privacy

Nothing watches you

No analytics, no telemetry in any app. The only network calls are license and update checks, plus a diagnostic report if you choose to send one. Details in the SideSchedule, TalkRemind and ToneCue privacy notes.

Install

Signed, notarized, updated

Notarized by Apple, updated through Sparkle. Or use Homebrew:
