What every app comes with.

Price

You pay once.

No subscription. After 14 free days the license is yours, for two Macs with SideSchedule or ToneCue and three with TalkRemind.

Account

There is none.

Nothing to sign up for. You enter a license key and that’s it.

Privacy

Nothing watches you.

No analytics, no telemetry in any app. The only network calls are license and update checks, plus a diagnostic report if you choose to send one. Details in the SideSchedule, TalkRemind and ToneCue privacy notes.

Install

Signed and notarized.

Notarized by Apple, updated through Sparkle. Or use Homebrew:

brew install --cask 4ingoj/tap/sideschedule
brew install --cask 4ingoj/tap/tonecue
