Questions

The ones that actually come up.

I already have a dictation app — why do I need this too?
Because owning it isn't the same as using it. Most people who buy a dictation app reach for it a fraction of the time they meant to, then fall back into typing out of habit. TalkRemind is the reminder that gets you to actually open it.
Does it read what I'm typing?
No. TalkRemind only observes keystroke timing through the macOS Accessibility API — when you type and how much, never the characters themselves.
What happens after the 14-day trial?
The nudges stop. Recording keeps running for free. Your dictation share and the 7-day trend stay visible, so you can watch the effect fade before deciding to buy.
Which dictation apps does it work with?
Seventeen are recognised on sight (full list above) and show their hotkey in the nudge automatically. Any other app with a hotkey works too — you enter its hotkey once and TalkRemind remembers it from then on. TalkRemind never transcribes anything itself — it only points you at the app that does.
How much time will this actually save me?
That depends on how much more you end up dictating — the app measures it instead of promising it. The short version is in “Your dictation app promised 4×” above: the speed-up only applies to what you actually speak. The stats screen shows your real share, so you can check it against your own week.
Can I turn it off for specific apps or times?
Yes. Exempt individual apps permanently, set a quiet-hours window, or pause everything for 30 minutes up to indefinitely, right from the menu bar.
Is my data sent anywhere?
No. Typing and dictation events stay on your Mac. The only network calls are license validation and update checks — no analytics, no tracking.
