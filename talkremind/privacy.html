Privacy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

TalkRemind is a macOS app made by Ingo Jurz, Graz, Austria. This page explains what data the app touches, what leaves your Mac, and what never does.

The short version

Typing and dictation events stay on your Mac. TalkRemind observes when you type and how much, never the characters. The app has no analytics and no tracking. Its only network calls are license validation (Lemon Squeezy) and update checks (Sparkle, via GitHub). Also, only if you choose to, the diagnostic report (see below).

Keystroke timing and Accessibility access

TalkRemind uses the macOS Accessibility API to see the timing and amount of your typing. It does not record or store the characters you type and does not read window contents. Your statistics (for example your dictation share and the seven-day trend) are stored locally on your Mac.

Optional iCloud sync

If you switch on iCloud sync, your statistics and settings (numbers, times, app names) are stored in your own iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit. They are not sent to us.

License activation and validation

When you enter a license key, TalkRemind contacts Lemon Squeezy to activate and validate it. The key is stored in the macOS Keychain. A network error never locks out a paying customer.

Updates

TalkRemind checks for updates through the Sparkle framework, which fetches an appcast file hosted on GitHub. GitHub may log your IP address as part of serving that file; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record. We receive the information Lemon Squeezy passes on for your order, such as your email address and country. Their privacy policy applies to the payment.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub processes server logs such as your IP address. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party. We count visits with GoatCounter (see “Visit statistics” below); apart from that script, this site loads no third-party scripts. If you arrive through a partner's link (a URL containing ?aff=), we add the partner's ID to the Buy links on that page, so Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider, can credit the partner if you buy. Reading the ID from the link stores nothing on your device. If you also agree in the notice, we keep the ID in your browser's local storage for 60 days, so the partner is credited for a later purchase too. When you start the app for the first time, it opens a welcome page on didact.digital once. If you had agreed, that page hands the ID to the app, which stores it in the macOS Keychain and adds it only to its Buy link. A click on a Buy link takes you to Lemon Squeezy, where their privacy policy applies. The legal basis for keeping the ID is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) TKG 2021). You can withdraw it at any time with the button below, which removes the stored ID from your browser. To have it removed from the app as well, write to hello@didact.digital. The app itself never loads a tracking script.

Visit statistics

This website counts visits with GoatCounter, an analytics service that works without cookies. We use it to see which pages are read, which links bring visitors here, and how often the download and purchase buttons are clicked. The data is not used for advertising and not to build profiles of individual people. When you open a page, your browser sends GoatCounter the page address, the referring site, the campaign tags in the link (for example utm_source), your browser and screen size, and your approximate location. According to GoatCounter, it stores no IP addresses or user-agent strings and sets no cookies, so you are not recognised across visits or across sites. A click on a download or purchase link is counted as an event with a fixed name, without anything about you. GoatCounter is run by Martin Tournoij (Ireland); its servers are at Hetzner Online GmbH in Finland and Germany. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding how the website is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time: hello@didact.digital. Blocking the script in your browser also works, and the site stays fully usable. The apps are separate: they contain no analytics and send no usage data.

Diagnostic report

In the app you can send a diagnostic report to didact. This only happens when you click “Send to didact” in the “Send diagnostic report” window. You see the complete text first. Nothing is transmitted without your click, and your consent covers that one report only.

What it contains: App, version, macOS version, Mac model, language, time, some app settings without secrets, the last lines of the app’s log and, if you enter them, your note and your email address. Your home folder appears as “~” in paths. The app does not write license keys, calendar titles, file contents or notes to its log.

What reports do not contain: No device or install ID, no account, no IP address. The report gets a random report ID, which you see after sending.

Purpose and legal basis: We use the report to find and fix the problem you reported, and we reply if you gave an address. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by clicking “Send to didact”.

Retention: Reports are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Deletion on request: Write to hello@didact.digital and name the report ID. We delete the report and confirm it. Without a report ID we cannot match a report to you, because we store no identifier.

Where the data is kept: With Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers and D1). The database is located in the EU. Cloudflare may process the request itself on its servers worldwide, at the network edge; the standard contractual clauses in the data processing agreement with Cloudflare apply. Cloudflare may keep request records in its own operating logs.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of data we hold about you by writing to hello@didact.digital. You can also complain to the Austrian data protection authority (Datenschutzbehörde, dsb.gv.at).
