Privacy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

ToneCue is a macOS app made by Ingo Jurz, Graz, Austria. This page explains what data the app touches, what leaves your Mac, and what never does.

The short version

ToneCue changes which audio devices macOS uses. It does not record, store or process your sound. The app has no account, no analytics and no tracking. Its only network calls are license validation (Lemon Squeezy) and update checks (Sparkle, via GitHub).

Audio devices

ToneCue reads the list of audio input and output devices and their names, and switches the system defaults. Your scenes, device names and settings are stored in a file on your Mac.

Calendar access (optional)

If you add a meeting trigger, macOS asks for calendar access. ToneCue reads event times and whether an event contains a meeting link, on your Mac, to decide when a meeting is running.

Other triggers

The app trigger checks which apps are running or in front. The microphone trigger checks whether any app has a microphone open. Neither reads what you do inside those apps.

License activation and validation

When you enter a license key, ToneCue contacts Lemon Squeezy to activate and validate it. The key is stored in the macOS Keychain. The 14-day trial start date is kept in the Keychain too. A network error never locks out a paying customer.

Updates

ToneCue checks for updates through the Sparkle framework, which fetches an appcast file hosted on GitHub. GitHub may log your IP address as part of serving that file; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record. We receive the information Lemon Squeezy passes on for your order, such as your email address and country. Their privacy policy applies to the payment.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub processes server logs such as your IP address. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party. We use no analytics, and this site loads no third-party scripts. If you arrive through a partner's link (a URL containing ?aff=), we add the partner's ID to the Buy links on that page, so Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider, can credit the partner if you buy. Reading the ID from the link stores nothing on your device. If you also agree in the notice, we keep the ID in your browser's local storage for 60 days, so the partner is credited for a later purchase too. When you start the app for the first time, it opens a welcome page on didact.digital once. If you had agreed, that page hands the ID to the app, which stores it in the macOS Keychain and adds it only to its Buy link. A click on a Buy link takes you to Lemon Squeezy, where their privacy policy applies. The legal basis for keeping the ID is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) TKG 2021). You can withdraw it at any time with the button below, which removes the stored ID from your browser. To have it removed from the app as well, write to hello@didact.digital. The app itself never loads a tracking script.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of data we hold about you by writing to hello@didact.digital. You can also complain to the Austrian data protection authority (Datenschutzbehörde, dsb.gv.at).
